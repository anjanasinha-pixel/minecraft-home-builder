How to Play

🎮 Movement

Use WASD keys or Arrow keys to move around. Press Space to jump!

🧱 Building

Look at a block and left-click to place a new block in front of it. Select different block types using the block selector at the bottom.

On mobile, tap the screen to place the currently selected block. Use the block buttons to choose dirt, grass, wood, stone, sand or brick first.

Important: After starting the game, click inside the game area so your mouse can move the camera. If you do not have a mouse, use Q and E to turn.

💥 Destroying

Look at a block and right-click to remove it. If you have no mouse, press O. On phone, tap the Remove button.

🆙 Jump

Press Space to jump on desktop. On phone, tap the Jump button.

👥 Co-op Play

Play with Reyansh! Each player controls their own avatar. Work together to build an amazing home!

🎨 Block Types

  • 🟤 Dirt - Basic building block
  • 🟩 Grass - Natural terrain
  • 🟫 Wood - Beautiful wooden structures
  • ⬜ Stone - Durable and strong
  • 🟨 Sand - Desert-like material
  • 🧱 Brick - Red brick for nice buildings
