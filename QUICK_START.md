# 🚀 Quick Start Guide

## Getting Started in 30 Seconds

1. **Open the Game**
   - Find the `index.html` file in the game folder
   - Double-click it or drag it into your web browser
   - The game will load and show a welcome screen

2. **Start Building**
   - Click "Start Game" button
   - Click on the game area to lock your mouse
   - You're now ready to build!

## First 5 Minutes

### Minute 1: Get Oriented
- Use WASD keys to walk around
- Move your mouse to look around
- Press Space to jump

### Minute 2: Select a Block
- Look at the block selector at bottom-left
- Click on a block type or press 1-6 on keyboard
- Let's start with **Dirt** (🟤)

### Minute 3: Place a Block
- Look at the ground in front of you
- **Left-click** to place a block
- Keep placing blocks to create a wall

### Minute 4: Remove Blocks
- Look at a block you just placed
- **Right-click** to remove it
- This lets you fix mistakes!

### Minute 5: Switch Materials
- Press **2** to select Grass
- Press **3** to select Wood
- Mix and match to create interesting patterns!

## Building Your First House

### Step 1: Foundation (5 min)
1. Find flat ground (or use the ground level)
2. Create a 6x6 or 8x8 square using Stone (press 4)
3. Place about 2-3 layers of blocks

### Step 2: Walls (10 min)
1. Switch to Brick (press 6)
2. Build walls around your foundation
3. Make walls about 3-4 blocks high
4. Leave space for a door (2 blocks wide)

### Step 3: Roof (5 min)
1. Switch to Wood (press 3)
2. Place blocks on top to create a roof
3. Make it sloped or flat - your choice!

### Step 4: Interior (10 min)
1. Switch to different blocks for decoration
2. Create rooms inside
3. Add details and furniture with Sand blocks

## Saving Your Work

✅ **Auto-Save**: Your world saves automatically

🔒 **Manual Save**: Press ESC → Click "Save World"

💾 **Load**: Your world appears when you restart the game

## Quick Fixes

### Stuck in a Block?
- Press Space to jump out
- Jump upward and forward

### Can't Place a Block?
- Make sure you're looking at an existing block
- Try clicking in front of (not inside) a block

### Performance Slow?
- Move to a different area with fewer blocks
- Your computer is rendering many blocks at once

### Lost?
- Look for the starting house (red brick structure)
- Press W to walk forward and explore

## Need Help?

- **Can't move?** - Make sure you clicked in the game first
- **Can't see blocks?** - Try pressing W to walk forward
- **Game lagging?** - Close other browser tabs
- **Need to pause?** - Press ESC key
- **Want to start over?** - Close browser and reopen
